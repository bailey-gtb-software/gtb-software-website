[ The problem ]

Three options. All of them broken.

Most businesses managing data have the same three choices. None of them are good enough.

Option 1

Spreadsheets

Fast to start. Painful at scale.

  • People spend up to 90% of their time working in Excel
  • 88% of all spreadsheets contain errors, with no way to audit
  • Data fragmented across files, tabs, and email threads
  • No access controls — anyone can overwrite anything
  • Zero automation — every update is a manual task

Option 2

Enterprise Software

Bloated features. Hidden costs. Long implementations.

  • Oversized packages with features you'll never use
  • Customisation costs exceed the original quote
  • 6–18 month implementations before you see value
  • $50,000+ before it fits how you actually work
  • Locked in — switching costs are brutal

Option 3

Custom Development

Built for you. Then one person leaves and no-one can maintain it.

  • Expensive to build, expensive to change
  • Dependent on one developer's knowledge
  • Every new requirement is a paid project
  • Technical debt compounds fast
  • Reliability not guaranteed

The middle ground

There's a better way.

Ragic looks like a spreadsheet, works like a proper relational database, and costs $5–$19 per user per month. No enterprise contract. No custom code. No spreadsheet chaos. GTB builds it for you, customises it to your workflow, and trains your team.

[ What Ragic does ]

A real database that your team
can actually use.

Business managers — not developers — are best positioned to build the tools their teams need. Ragic is the platform that makes that possible. Spreadsheet-like interface on the surface. Proper relational database underneath.

Linked Records

Connect customers to orders to invoices to jobs — everything linked, nothing duplicated. Like a proper database, without writing a line of SQL.

Approval Workflows

Build multi-step approval chains into any process. Purchase orders, leave requests, job sign-offs — routed automatically, tracked completely.

Mobile Access + Offline Sync

iOS and Android apps with offline mode. Your field team can fill in job reports without Wi-Fi — it syncs when they're back online.

Role-Based Access Control

Define exactly who can view, edit, or delete each record. Employees see what they need. Nothing more. Full audit trail of every change.

Automated Alerts & Reminders

Email and push notifications triggered by data conditions — overdue tasks, low stock, expiring contracts. No manual chasing.

Reporting: Gantt, Pivot, Calendar

Built-in report types that work out of the box. Gantt charts for project timelines. Pivot tables for analysis. Calendar views for scheduling.

Excel Import/Export

Migration is painless. Import your existing spreadsheet data directly. Export to Excel or Word at any point. No lock-in.

API + Zapier / Make / n8n

Connect Ragic to the rest of your stack. Native connectors for Zapier, Make, and n8n. Full REST API for anything else. GTB handles the integrations.

Built-In AI Agent

Ragic's AI has full context of your actual business data — not a generic assistant. Runs 24/7. Built for sustained operations, not just demos.

40+ Pre-Built Templates

CRM, inventory, HR, project management, field service — a template for every common use case. GTB starts from the closest match and customises it to your workflow.
