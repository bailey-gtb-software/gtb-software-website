[ The problem ]

The document exists.
No one can find the answer.

Every organisation with a rulebook, compliance code, or policy manual runs into the same three problems.

Problem 1

The PDF is 400 pages

Staff reference the document constantly but searching a PDF is slow and error-prone. Two people look up the same rule and walk away with different answers. Nobody has time to read it end to end.

Problem 2

Institutional knowledge leaves with people

The senior umpire, compliance officer, or certifier who "just knows" the rules isn't always available. When they leave, that knowledge walks out the door — and the organisation is back to square one.

Problem 3

Inconsistent rulings, no audit trail

Without a single authoritative source, decisions vary by person, by day, and by who got asked. Disputes escalate. Liability accumulates. Nobody can point to where the decision came from.

The fix

One assistant. Your document.
Every question answered with the exact rule cited.

The AI only answers from your content — no hallucinations outside the corpus. Every answer includes the exact clause. Every question is logged. The assistant gets smarter about your document over time.

[ Cricket Tasmania — Laws of Cricket AI Assistant ]

The problem

Cricket Tasmania runs 507 senior teams and 8,750 registered players across 150+ clubs. Every season, CT staff fielded 150+ by-law queries — each taking an average of 30 minutes to research and respond. Rulings were inconsistent across clubs. There was no precedent library, no audit trail, and no way for umpires or club admins to get reliable answers outside business hours.

The build

A mobile-first AI assistant trained on three layers of cricket law: ICC Laws of Cricket, Cricket Australia playing conditions, and CT's own by-laws and local conditions. Hybrid search — semantic vector retrieval plus full-text matching — fused to surface the most relevant rules. Every answer cites the exact clause. Umpires log in via phone OTP. When a question needs a formal ruling, they escalate through the app — CT admins receive the full context, draft a ruling, and it's published as a precedent for all future queries.

The outcome

75 hrs of staff time freed per season — returned to competition development, high performance, and CTPL programs
507 clubs now getting consistent answers from the same single source of truth — no more variation by who gets asked
24/7 availability — umpires get answers on match day, evenings, weekends, whenever they need them
∞ precedent library — every ruling is captured and becomes a searchable resource for future queries
